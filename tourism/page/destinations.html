Top Destinations in Sri Lanka

Explore the most beautiful and captivating places in Sri Lanka

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

Ancient rock fortress with stunning frescoes and panoramic views. A UNESCO World Heritage Site dating back to the 5th century.

📍 Location: Central Province

Highlights: Ancient palace ruins, frescoes, gardens

Ella

Scenic hill country town surrounded by tea plantations, waterfalls, and stunning mountain views.

📍 Location: Uva Province

Highlights: Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adams Peak, Ravana Falls

Mirissa Beach

Beautiful beach perfect for whale watching, surfing, and enjoying pristine golden sands.

📍 Location: Southern Province

Highlights: Blue whale watching, surfing, beach parties

Kandy

The cultural capital of Sri Lanka, home to the sacred Temple of the Tooth and beautiful Kandy Lake.

📍 Location: Central Province

Highlights: Temple of the Tooth, Kandy Lake, cultural shows

Yala National Park

Premier wildlife safari destination with the highest density of leopards in the world.

📍 Location: Southern Province

Highlights: Leopards, elephants, diverse wildlife

Galle Fort

Historic Dutch colonial fort with well-preserved architecture, museums, and charming cafes.

📍 Location: Southern Province

Highlights: Colonial architecture, lighthouse, museums
